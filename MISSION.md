# Mission: ECON2103 midterm

## Why
Prepare for the multiple-choice midterm by understanding and applying Chapters 1–8, 14 and 15 from the Fall 2026 course materials.

## Success looks like
- Explain key concepts and diagrams in English and Chinese.
- Solve graph, calculation, policy and competitive-firm questions without notes.
- Identify misunderstandings through retrieval and revisit them.

## Constraints
- Concise, bilingual, chapter-based and visually clear; use the current course slides and problem sets as scope authority.
- Keep practice sessions short. Mastery is unverified until demonstrated.

## Out of scope
- Claiming predicted exam questions or scores; presenting optional or excluded material as confirmed required content.
