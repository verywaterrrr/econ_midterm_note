Revenue and opportunity costs / 收入與機會成本~TR equals price times quantity. Explicit costs require monetary payments; implicit costs are forgone alternatives for owned resources, such as the owner's wage or interest income. Economic costs include both.~總收入為 P×Q。顯性成本需付款；隱性成本是自有資源放棄的其他收益，例如老闆工資及資金利息。經濟成本包含兩者。~Example 例：Sell 100 items at $5: TR=500. Rent $100 is explicit; forgone owner wages $150 are implicit. 成本性質取決於有否付款。~Owner invests savings that could earn $200 interest. This forgone interest is? 放棄利息屬什麼？~Implicit cost / 隱性成本^Sales revenue / 銷售收入^No economic cost / 無經濟成本^Necessarily a cash expense / 必現金支出~0~Using owned funds sacrifices their next-best return.~
Accounting and economic profit / 會計與經濟利潤~Accounting profit subtracts explicit costs from revenue. Economic profit also subtracts implicit costs. Zero economic profit covers all opportunity costs, including the owner's normal return.~會計利潤扣顯性成本；經濟利潤再扣隱性成本。零經濟利潤已補償老闆等自有資源的機會成本。~Example 例：TR=1,000, explicit=600, implicit=300. Accounting profit=400, economic profit=100. 兩種利潤相差隱性成本。~TR=500, explicit=300, implicit=200. Economic profit? 經濟利潤？~0^200^300^500~0~500−300−200=0, although accounting profit is 200.~
Marginal cost from total cost / 從總成本求邊際成本~First convert average costs back to total costs when necessary, then take ΔTC/ΔQ. The extra unit's cost can be much larger than either the average cost or the change in average cost.~先把平均成本乘產量還原總成本，再用 ΔTC/ΔQ。額外單位成本可遠高於平均成本或平均成本增幅。~Example 例：Q=600,ATC=300 gives TC=180,000; Q=601,ATC=301 gives TC=180,901. The extra unit costs 901.~Q=10,ATC=5; Q=11,ATC=6. MC of unit 11? 第十一件 MC？~1^6^11^16~3~11×6−10×5=16. Compare an extra order's revenue with 16.~
Marginal product / 邊際產量~MP is the change in output divided by the change in an input. It can initially rise with specialization and later fall as workers share fixed equipment. A production function becomes flatter when marginal product diminishes.~邊際產量為產量變化除投入變化。分工可能先提高 MP，但工人共享固定設備後 MP 可遞減；生產函數隨之變平。~Example 例：Output with 0,1,2,3 workers is 0,10,25,35. MPs are 10,15,10: first rising, then diminishing.~Output rises from 30 to 42 when labor rises from 3 to 4. MP? 邊際產量多少？~3^4^12^42~2~ΔQ/ΔL=(42−30)/(4−3)=12.~production
Marginal product and cost / 邊際產量與成本~With a fixed wage w and labor as the changing input, marginal cost is approximately w/MP. Lower marginal product means more labor per extra unit, raising MC and making total cost steeper as output expands.~工資 w 固定、只調勞工時，MC 約為 w/MP。MP 下降使每件需要更多勞工，MC 上升，總成本曲線變陡。~Example 例：A $100 worker adds 20 units, costing $5 per extra unit; if the next adds 10, incremental cost becomes $10 per unit. 邊際產量減半，單位額外成本加倍。~Wage=60 and MP=12. MC? 邊際成本？~5^12^60^720~0~w/MP=60/12=5.~
Fixed, variable and total cost / 固定、可變與總成本~Fixed cost does not vary with current output in the relevant short run. Variable cost does; TC=FC+VC. At zero output, VC is zero in the standard schedules, so TC(0) identifies FC.~固定成本在相關短期不隨產量改變，可變成本則會。TC=FC+VC；標準表中零產量的 VC=0，因此 TC(0)=FC。~Example 例：TC at zero is 30. At Q=5, TC=80, so FC=30 and VC=50.~FC=40 and TC=110. VC? 可變成本？~40^70^110^150~1~VC=TC−FC=110−40=70.~
Average and marginal formulas / 平均與邊際公式~AFC=FC/Q, AVC=VC/Q, ATC=TC/Q=AFC+AVC. MC=ΔTC/ΔQ=ΔVC/ΔQ when FC stays fixed. Average costs are undefined at zero quantity.~平均成本以各項總成本除產量，ATC=AFC+AVC。FC 不變時，MC 也可由 VC 的變化計算。Q=0 時不能計平均成本。~Example 例：At Q=4,FC=20,VC=28: AFC=5,AVC=7,ATC=12. If Q=5 has TC=60, MC=(60−48)/1=12.~FC=12,VC=18,Q=3. ATC? 平均總成本？~4^6^10^30~2~(12+18)/3=10.~
Cost curve relationships / 成本線關係~AFC falls as a fixed sum is spread across output. When MC is below an average it pulls that average down; above it, the average rises. Thus MC crosses AVC and ATC at their respective minima for smooth typical curves.~固定成本分攤更多產量令 AFC 下降；MC 低於平均值會拉低平均，高於平均值會拉高平均。因此 MC 在 AVC、ATC 各自最低點相交。~Example 例：Current ATC=10; the next unit costs 6. Adding a below-average unit lowers ATC. 下一件較便宜會拉低平均。~At a given output MC<ATC. What is ATC doing locally? 平均總成本如何變？~Falling / 下降^Rising / 上升^Always at minimum / 必在最低點^Undefined / 未定義~0~A below-average marginal unit pulls the average down.~cost
Efficient scale / 有效率規模~Efficient scale is the output minimizing ATC. Read the lowest ATC point or compare ATC across a schedule. It is distinct from maximum output and from the minimum AVC shutdown threshold.~有效率規模是 ATC 最低的產量，可由圖最低點或表格比較找出。它與最大產量及 AVC 最低點不同。~Example 例：ATC at Q=1,2,3,4 is 12,8,7,8. Efficient scale is Q=3. 第三單位產量平均成本最低。~ATC values at Q=2,3,4 are 9,6,7. Efficient scale among these choices? 有效率規模？~Q=2^Q=3^Q=4^Q=9~1~The minimum listed ATC is 6 at Q=3.~cost
Lump-sum versus unit tax / 固定總額稅與單位稅~A fixed lump-sum tax increases FC, AFC and ATC but leaves VC, AVC and MC unchanged. A per-unit tax adds to VC and raises AVC, ATC and MC, leaving FC and AFC unchanged.~固定總額稅增加 FC、AFC、ATC，不改 VC、AVC、MC；每件稅增加 VC、AVC、ATC、MC，不改 FC、AFC。~Example 例：A $100 firm tax adds 100/Q to ATC. A $2 output tax adds $2 to AVC,ATC and MC. 前者隨產量分攤，後者每件相同。~Which curve stays unchanged after a fixed lump-sum tax? 固定總額稅後何線不變？~ATC^AFC^MC^Total fixed cost~2~The tax does not alter the extra cost of another unit.~
Short run and long run / 短期與長期~In the short run, some inputs or plant scale are fixed. In the long run, all inputs can be adjusted, including entry, exit and plant size. The relevant horizon depends on how long adjustments take, not a universal number of months.~短期有部分投入或規模固定；長期可改全部投入，包括廠房和退出。長短期由調整所需時間決定，沒有通用月份界線。~Example 例：A bakery can hire overtime this week but needs months to install a larger oven. The oven is fixed in the short run.~Which is possible in the long run? 長期可以怎樣？~Adjust plant size / 調整廠房規模^Only change labor / 只調勞工^No entry or exit / 不可進出^All costs stay fixed / 所有成本固定~0~All inputs become adjustable over the long-run horizon.~
Scale economies / 規模經濟~Economies of scale mean LRATC falls as output grows, often through specialization. Constant returns to scale mean LRATC is unchanged; diseconomies mean it rises, possibly because coordination becomes harder. Distinguish this from diminishing MP with fixed inputs.~規模經濟是長期 ATC 隨產量下降；不變規模報酬是 ATC 持平；規模不經濟是 ATC 上升，可能來自協調困難。這與固定投入下 MP 遞減不同。~Example 例：Doubling all inputs doubles output: unchanged cost per unit. If coordination makes costs rise faster than output, LRATC rises.~Output expands and LRATC falls. What is present? 產量增加、長期平均成本下降？~Economies of scale / 規模經濟^Diseconomies of scale / 規模不經濟^Zero marginal cost necessarily / MC 必零^A fixed short-run input by definition / 定義上固定投入~0~Falling LRATC is the definition of economies of scale.~scale
