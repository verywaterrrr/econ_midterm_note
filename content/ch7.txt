Willingness to pay and CS / 願付價格與消費者剩餘~Willingness to pay is a buyer's maximum valuation for a unit. Buy units valued at least as highly as the price. Consumer surplus sums WTP−price over units purchased; the demand curve shows the marginal unit's valuation.~願付價格是每單位最高估值。只買價值至少等於價格的單位；CS 把已購單位的價值減付款加總，需求線顯示邊際買家估值。~Example 例：WTP for three units is 7,5,3 and P=4. Costs and PS / 成本與生產者剩餘~A seller's cost measures the opportunity cost of supplying a unit. Producer surplus sums price−cost over units sold; supply gives the marginal seller's cost. With fixed costs, PS generally differs from profit by fixed cost.~賣方成本是供應單位的機會成本。PS 是售出單位的價格減成本總和；供給線顯示邊際成本。存在固定成本時，PS 通常等於利潤加固定成本。~Example 例：Unit costs 1,3,5 and P=4. Sell two; PS=(4−1)+(4−3)=4.~Unit costs 2,4,7 and P=6. PS? 成本二、四、七，價格六？~2^4^6^8~2~Sell the first two: (6−2)+(6−4)=6.~

Fixed fee versus per use / 固定費與按次收費~Choose usage using the marginal price, then subtract all payments including fixed fees from total willingness to pay. A fixed fee affects whether to subscribe, while a zero per-use price can increase usage after subscribing.~使用量取決於邊際價格；比較整體淨收益時要扣所有付款，包括固定費。固定費影響是否訂閱，訂閱後的零邊際費可增加使用。~Example 例：Demand Q=10−P. Total surplus / 總剩餘~In an untaxed market, payments transfer value between buyer and seller. Adding CS and PS cancels the payment, leaving total value to buyers minus total opportunity cost to sellers.~無稅市場內，買方付款等於賣方收入，兩者相加抵消；總剩餘是買方總價值減賣方總機會成本。~Example 例：WTP=20, price=12, cost=7. CS=8, PS=5, total=13=20−7. 價格影響分配但這筆交易總收益為十三。~WTP=30, price=18, cost=10. Total surplus? 總剩餘？~8^12^20^30~2~CS=12 plus PS=8 gives 20; also 30−10=20.~
Efficient equilibrium / 有效率均衡~In a competitive market without relevant market failures, trade units for which marginal buyer value exceeds marginal cost. At equilibrium, marginal value equals marginal cost; increasing or decreasing quantity from that point cannot raise total surplus.~沒有相關市場失靈的競爭模型中，應完成邊際價值高於成本的交易。均衡使兩者相等，偏離該數量會失去總剩餘。~Example 例：An extra unit valued at $9 costs $6. Making it adds $3 surplus, so output below that unit leaves gains unrealized. 這項交易增加三元總收益。~Marginal value is $8 and marginal cost $5. Underproduction and overproduction / 過少與過多生產~Below efficient quantity, some omitted units have value above cost, creating unrealized gains. Above it, extra units cost more than their value. Efficiency assumptions / 效率假設~Efficiency maximizes total surplus; equality concerns distribution. The competitive-efficiency result assumes conditions such as price taking and no relevant external effects. Market power or externalities can break the result.~效率最大化總剩餘，平等關心分配。市場效率結論依賴價格接受及無相關外部性等條件；壟斷力量或外部影響可破壞結論。~Example 例：A market may maximize buyer-plus-seller surplus yet impose pollution costs on residents. Insurance and excessive use / 保險與過量使用~When a patient's out-of-pocket price is below the true marginal social cost, the patient may choose units valued above their own payment but below society's cost. Fixed premiums do not add to the marginal price of a procedure.~病人自付低於社會邊際成本時，可能使用價值高於自付但低於成本的服務。保費若不隨使用次數改變，不增加每項服務的邊際價格。~Example 例：A procedure worth $60 costs society $100; the insured patient pays $20.
