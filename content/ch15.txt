Competitive assumptions / 競爭市場假設~Many buyers and sellers trade identical products, so an individual firm takes the market price as given. Free entry and exit is a long-run assumption that permits industry adjustment when profits change.~許多買賣雙方交易同質產品，個別企業無法自行影響市價。自由進出是長期假設，使利潤吸引進入或虧損引致退出。~Example 例：One small wheat farmer cannot raise the market wheat price by choosing a personal price. 新企業能進入時，長期正利潤會吸引供給。~A price-taking firm faces which demand for its output? 價格接受者面對的個別需求？~Horizontal at market price / 市價水平線^The entire downward market demand / 整體市場需求^Vertical at zero / 零量垂直線^An upward labor supply curve / 勞工供給線~0~It can sell relevant quantities at the given market price; individual and market demand differ.~

Shutdown rule / 停產法則~In the short run, fixed costs remain payable. Shut down if the best operating revenue cannot cover variable cost: TR<VC, or P<AVC at that candidate output. At the threshold, operating and shutdown can give equal profit.~短期停產仍要付固定成本。最佳營運方案若 TR<VC，等同該產量 P<AVC，就應停產；臨界相等時兩者無差異。~Example 例：TR=80,VC=100,FC=50. Operating loses 70; shutdown loses 50. Stop production for the period.~P=4,AVC=5 at the best operating candidate. Operating at a loss / 虧損下營運~A loss does not by itself imply shutdown. If revenue covers VC and some FC, operating reduces the loss compared with paying all FC without production. Long-run entry and exit / 長期進入與退出~With all costs avoidable in the long run, persistent P<ATC creates an exit incentive. Positive economic profit attracts entry where entry is free. At P=ATC, normal opportunity costs are covered and economic profit is zero.~長期全部成本可調整，持續 P<ATC 會引致退出；自由進入下正經濟利潤吸引新企業。P=ATC 已涵蓋正常機會成本。~Example 例：P=8 and minimum sustainable ATC=10: remaining indefinitely gives a negative economic return, encouraging exit.~In the long run P>ATC with free entry. Firm supply thresholds / 企業供給門檻~Short-run supply follows the rising MC branch above minimum AVC; lower prices imply zero output. The textbook long-run supply rule uses rising MC at or above minimum ATC when all costs are avoidable. At P=4, the firm may operate in the short run yet prefer exit in the long run.~Price is below minimum AVC. Market equilibrium with N firms / N 家企業的市場均衡~Sum each firm's q(P) horizontally. With identical firms, market supply is Nq(P). Equate this to market demand, solve P, then compute q and profit using the firm's own costs.~先以 Nq(P) 得市場供給，再與市場需求相等求價。個別產量與利潤必須用企業自己的成本計算。~Example 例：TC=50+q²/2,MC=q, N=9; Qd=120−P. Each q=P, so 9P=120−P gives P=12,Q=108,q=12; profit=144−122=22.~Four identical firms each supply q=P. Demand Qd=100−P. Market price? 四家各供 q=P，市場價？~10^20^25^50~1~4P=100−P gives P=20 and market Q=80.~
Entry and supply adjustment / 進入與供給調整~Positive profit induces entry, shifting market supply right and lowering price; losses induce exit, shifting supply left and raising price, holding demand and relevant costs fixed. Adjustment continues until entry or exit incentives disappear.~需求及相關成本不變下，正利潤吸引進入使市場供給右移、價跌；虧損引致退出使供給左移、價升，直到進出誘因消失。~Example 例：A profitable wheat industry attracts farmers. Greater market supply reduces price and each firm's economic profit. 更多企業分攤市場需求。~Firms incur persistent losses and exit. Long-run equilibrium and firm count / 長期均衡與企業數~For identical firms with free entry and constant input costs, long-run equilibrium has P=MC=min ATC and zero economic profit. Demand changes across horizons / 需求變化的短長期~A demand increase raises price and operating output in the short run with a fixed number of firms. Profits then attract entry. With identical constant-cost firms, long-run price returns to minimum ATC while market quantity and firm count rise. Limited inputs or differing firm costs can make LR supply upward sloping.~需求增加先令短期價、每家產量及利潤升；長期進入擴大供給。相同固定成本行業長期價回最低 ATC，總量及企業數增加；投入稀缺或成本不同可令長期供給向上。~Example 例：Long-run price is 10 and firm q=10. Input costs, technology and patents / 投入成本、技術與專利~An input-price increase raises relevant variable costs and MC, shifting market supply left. Technology can lower costs; trace the short-run price and profits before deciding entry or exit. A patent can keep an innovator's advantage from being copied until it expires.~投入價升提高相關 VC、MC，令市場供給左移。技術進步可降成本；先求短期價與利潤再判斷進出。專利可暫時阻止其他企業複製優勢。~Example 例：One firm's patented process lowers its cost while market price barely changes. It earns extra profit; after expiry, broad adoption can lower market price. 專利到期後優勢可能擴散。~A patented cost reduction is available only to one small firm.
