Midpoint elasticity / 中點法彈性~Price elasticity of demand is the absolute percentage quantity change divided by percentage price change. Use the average of the two endpoints as each denominator so reversing the direction gives the same result.~需求價格彈性用數量百分比變化除價格百分比變化，再取絕對值。中點法以兩點平均數為分母，避免方向影響結果。~Example 例：P:10→12, Q:100→80. Ed=(20/90)/(2/11)=11/9≈1.22. 需求有彈性。~P:4→6, Q:12→8. Midpoint Ed? 中點法彈性？~0.5^1^2^4~1~(4/10)/(2/5)=1.~
Classifying elasticity / 彈性分類~Ed>1 is elastic, Ed<1 inelastic, and Ed=1 unit elastic. Vertical demand is perfectly inelastic with Ed=0. Horizontal demand is perfectly elastic in the idealized limit. Classify using percentage responses, not visual angle alone.~Ed 大於一有彈性，小於一缺乏彈性，等於一為單位彈性。垂直需求完全無彈性；水平需求完全有彈性。圖線斜度還受單位及軸比例影響。~Example 例：A 10% price rise reduces Q by 3%, so Ed=0.3 and demand is inelastic. 價升一成、量跌百分之三。~A perfectly vertical demand curve has which elasticity? 垂直需求彈性？~0^1^2^Infinity / 無限大~0~Quantity does not respond to price. 數量不隨價格變。~extremes
Determinants of elasticity / 彈性決定因素~Close substitutes make switching easier; narrowly defined goods tend to have more substitutes. Necessities tend to be less elastic than luxuries, and longer adjustment time generally increases responsiveness.~替代品多便容易轉換；商品定義較窄通常有更多替代品。必需品通常較缺乏彈性，時間較長通常更能調整。~Example 例：Demand for one brand of bottled water is likely more elastic than demand for drinking water overall. 單一品牌比整體食水更易被替代。~Which demand is usually more elastic, other things equal? 一般何者更有彈性？~One coffee brand / 單一咖啡品牌^All beverages / 所有飲料^Essential emergency care / 必需急救^A good with no substitutes / 無替代品商品~0~A narrowly defined brand has many close alternatives.~
Elasticity versus slope / 彈性與斜率~Slope uses absolute changes in P and Q; elasticity uses percentage changes. On linear demand Q=a−bP, Ed=bP/Q. It is higher near the high-price, low-quantity end even though slope is constant.~斜率用單位變化，彈性用百分比。線性需求 Q=a−bP 的 Ed=bP/Q；高價低量端彈性大，斜率仍相同。~Example 例：For Q=100−P, Ed at P=80,Q=20 is 4; at P=20,Q=80 it is 0.25. 同線不同點的彈性不同。~For Q=100−P at P=50,Q=50, Ed is? 彈性多少？~0.5^1^2^50~1~Ed=1×50/50=1.~
Revenue test / 總收入判斷~Along a fixed demand curve, a price rise raises total revenue when demand is inelastic and lowers it when demand is elastic. Reverse the direction for a price cut. For large changes crossing elasticity regions, calculate P×Q directly.~沿固定需求線，缺乏彈性時價升令收入增；有彈性時價升令收入減。減價時反向；大幅變動跨越不同彈性區域時直接算 P×Q。~Example 例：P rises from 5 to 6, Q falls from 5 to 4.5. Revenue rises from 25 to 27; the quantity response is relatively small. 價升幅度勝過量跌。~Along an elastic demand segment, a small price cut causes TR to do what? 有彈性需求區減價？~Rise / 上升^Fall / 下降^Always stay fixed / 必不變^Become negative / 變負~0~The percentage quantity gain exceeds the percentage price reduction.~inelastic
Fixed quantity or spending / 固定數量或支出~A buyer who always buys the same quantity has zero price elasticity. A buyer who always spends the same amount has Q=k/P, giving unit elasticity. These are statements about behavior across prices.~每次購買固定數量代表零彈性；每次固定支出時 Q=k/P，代表單位彈性。須看價格改變時的購買規則。~Example 例：“Five liters regardless of price” means Ed=0. “$20 of fuel regardless of price” means Ed=1. 固定公升與固定金額不同。~A buyer always buys $30 of rice. Price elasticity? 每次買三十元米？~0^0.5^1^Infinity / 無限大~2~Fixed expenditure means P×Q is constant, so demand is unit elastic.~
Income & cross-price elasticity / 收入與交叉彈性~Income elasticity is %ΔQd/%Δincome: positive for normal goods, negative for inferior goods. Cross-price elasticity is %ΔQd of A/%ΔP of B: positive for substitutes, negative for complements.~收入彈性正值屬正常品，負值屬劣等品。交叉價格彈性正值屬替代品，負值屬互補品；計算時保留正負號。~Example 例：Tea price rises 10%, coffee demand rises 5%. Cross elasticity=+0.5, suggesting substitutes. 咖啡需求隨茶價同向。~Income rises 10%, demand falls 5%. Income elasticity and classification? 收入增、需求減？~−0.5, inferior / 劣等品^+0.5, normal / 正常品^−2, substitute / 替代品^+2, complement / 互補品~0~−5%/10%=−0.5; demand falls with income.~
Supply elasticity / 供給彈性~Supply elasticity is the percentage quantity supplied response divided by the percentage price change. A longer horizon often permits new capacity, entry or changed inputs, making supply more elastic; this is a tendency, not a universal law.~供給彈性用供給量百分比反應除價格百分比變化。長期可改變設備、投入或企業數目，通常更有彈性。~Example 例：P rises 10%, Qs rises 20%; Es=2. Firms may respond more over a year than over a day. 供給反應大於價格變化。~A 5% price rise increases supply by 15%. Es? 供給彈性？~1/3^1^3^10~2~15%/5%=3.~
Elasticity and shock size / 彈性與衝擊反應~After a curve shifts, the elasticity of the other curve shapes the price and quantity adjustment. A less responsive demand curve generally produces a larger price change and smaller quantity change for a comparable supply shock.~一條曲線移動時，另一條線的彈性影響新均衡。相同供給衝擊下，需求較缺乏彈性通常令價格變較多、數量變較少。~Example 例：A supply cut in essential fuel can cause a sharp price rise in the short run when demand barely responds. 短期必需燃料難以減用。~Same supply reduction, which demand gives the larger price rise? 同樣供給減少，哪種需求令價升較多？~More inelastic / 較缺乏彈性^More elastic / 較有彈性^Perfectly horizontal / 完全水平^Elasticity cannot matter / 彈性無影響~0~Less quantity adjustment requires more price adjustment.~
Crop and restriction applications / 農作物與限制供給~A broad supply increase can lower revenue when market demand is inelastic. A local producer faces more substitutes for its output, so a local crop loss can reduce its revenue. Restricting oil or drug supply can raise spending when demand is inelastic; linked complements may also be affected.~整體豐收可能因需求缺乏彈性而令總收入減少；局部農民面對其他地區替代品，失收可能降低自身收入。限制油或毒品供給可令價格及支出上升。~Example 例：Worldwide grain supply expands; price falls sharply and purchases rise slightly, reducing total farm revenue. 全球穀物豐收可能令農民總收入下降。~Supply of an inelastically demanded drug falls. User spending may do what? 毒品供給減、需求缺乏彈性，支出可能？~Rise / 上升^Always fall / 必下降^Stay fixed by definition / 定義上固定^Equal zero / 等於零~0~The price increase can outweigh the quantity reduction along demand.~inelastic
Price changes with fixed quantity / 價變但量不變~A demand increase with vertical supply can raise price without changing quantity. A supply decrease with vertical demand can do the same. Opposing quantity effects from simultaneous shifts are another possibility.~需求增配合垂直供給可令價升量不變；供給減配合垂直需求也可。雙線移動的數量效果互相抵消也是可能原因。~Example 例：A venue has a fixed 1,000 seats. Increased demand can raise ticket price while seats sold remain 1,000. 座位供應固定。~P rises while Q is unchanged. Which explanation is possible? 價升量不變何解釋可能？~Demand rises with vertical supply / 需求增、供給垂直^Supply rises with horizontal demand / 供給增、需求水平^Demand falls with upward supply / 需求跌、供給向上^Supply rises with downward demand / 供給增、需求向下~0~Vertical supply fixes quantity despite a demand shift.~extremes
