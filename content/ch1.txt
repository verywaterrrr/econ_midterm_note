Scarcity & trade-offs / 稀缺與取捨~Scarcity means available resources cannot satisfy every want. A constraint limits feasible choices. Choosing one use of time or money gives up other possible uses.~稀缺同時涉及有限資源和人們的需要。預算、時間和能力限制可行選項；做選擇就要放棄其他用途。~Example 例：You have 2 hours; studying takes 2 hours and working takes 2 hours. You can do either, but cannot do both. 只有兩小時，讀書和打工各要兩小時，兩者不能同時完成。~You have $100; two activities each cost $80. What creates the trade-off? 兩項活動各要 $80，取捨來自什麼？~Limited budget / 預算有限^No preferences / 沒有喜好^Unlimited resources / 資源無限^No alternatives / 沒有選項~0~The budget prevents choosing both. 預算限制使你必須取捨。~
Opportunity cost / 機會成本~Count the resources actually sacrificed plus the value of the best forgone alternative. When alternatives are mutually exclusive, their values are not added together. Avoid counting the same cost twice.~機會成本包括實際犧牲的金錢與最佳放棄選項的價值。互斥選項只取最佳一個，避免重複計算。~Example 例：A free study session replaces a $200 job or a free outing worth $120. Opportunity cost = max(200,120) = $200. 免費讀書活動的最佳替代方案是賺 $200。~A $50 concert replaces a job earning $100 or a free walk worth $30. Opportunity cost? $50 演唱會取代賺 $100 的工作或價值 $30 的散步，成本多少？~$50^$100^$150^$180~2~$50 ticket plus $100 best alternative = $150. 門票加最佳放棄收入，散步不另加。~
Valuing alternatives / 比較方案價值~Put benefits and costs on a common scale using willingness to pay. Compare the net benefit of each feasible option, including time and forgone income. A benefit can have economic value without being cash income.~可用願付價格衡量快樂或休息等非金錢收益。把每個方案的收益減去相關成本，並包括放棄收入，再比較淨收益。~Example 例：A trip brings enjoyment valued at $500, costs $200 and replaces $150 work. Net advantage over work = 500−200−150 = $150. 旅遊的額外淨收益為 $150。~Enjoyment is worth $300; ticket costs $100; forgone wages are $250. Net advantage of attending? 參加活動的淨優勢？~+$150^+$50^−$50^−$150~2~300−100−250 = −50. Attending gives less value than the work alternative.~
Sunk cost / 沉沒成本~An unrecoverable past cost cannot change with today's decision. Compare the future benefit and future cost of continuing; include any recoverable resale value that would be sacrificed.~無法收回的過去支出不隨今天的選擇改變。繼續與否只比較未來相關成本及收益；若可出售資產，放棄的售價仍有關。~Example 例：Already spent $5m; finishing costs $1m and yields $3m. Continue because 3−1 = +$2m. 已花 $5m 不影響現在完成的額外淨收益。~Past research cost $8m; completion costs $2m; new revenue $1.5m. What should the firm do? 應否完成？~Continue; revenue is positive / 繼續^Stop; future net benefit is negative / 停止^Continue; past cost is large / 繼續^Stop only if past cost is zero / 過去成本零才停止~1~1.5−2 = −$0.5m. Continuing reduces wealth further. 繼續會額外損失 $0.5m。~
Marginal reasoning / 邊際決策~Marginal means additional. Take the next unit when its extra benefit exceeds its extra cost. Total or average benefits cannot by themselves determine whether one more unit is worthwhile.~邊際是額外一單位的變化。下一單位的收益高於成本就值得；總收益或平均收益不足以決定是否多做一單位。~Example 例：A third study hour adds 8 expected marks and costs leisure valued at 5 mark-equivalents. Extra net gain = 3, so study that hour. 第三小時額外淨收益為 3。~An extra unit brings $12 revenue and $15 additional cost. Should output increase? 多做一件是否值得？~Yes; total sales rise / 值得^Yes; average cost may fall / 值得^No; marginal net benefit is −$3 / 不值得^No; all production is unprofitable / 全部停產~2~12−15 = −3 for this additional unit; this does not establish the profitability of all output.~
Incentives / 激勵~A reward or penalty changes the relative payoff from an action. Predict the response at the affected margin, allowing for unintended consequences and differences between individuals.~獎勵與懲罰會改變行動的相對收益。要辨認哪個選擇的成本或收益改變，並考慮行為反應及非預期效果。~Example 例：A charge on plastic bags raises the cost of using each bag, encouraging shoppers to bring reusable bags. 膠袋費改變每次使用膠袋的成本。~A parking fine increases, with enforcement unchanged. What incentive changes? 執法不變但罰款增加，誘因如何變？~Illegal parking becomes more costly / 違泊成本上升^Legal parking becomes prohibited / 合法泊車被禁^All drivers stop driving / 所有人停駕^The expected penalty falls / 預期罰則下降~0~A larger fine raises the expected cost of a violation, holding detection probability fixed.~
Specialization & exchange / 專門化與交換~Specialization lets people concentrate on activities with lower relative opportunity cost. Voluntary exchange can then give both a more valuable consumption bundle. Gains depend on the trade terms and available alternatives.~專門化讓人集中於機會成本較低的活動。適當交易條件下，雙方都可得到比自行生產更好的消費組合。~Example 例：A gives up 2 meals to make a shirt; B gives up 4. A sells a shirt for 3 meals: A gains 1 meal and B saves 1 meal. 一件衫換三餐令雙方獲益。~A's cost of a shirt is 2 meals; B's is 4. Which trade price benefits both strictly? 何種價格令雙方嚴格得益？~1 meal / 一餐^2 meals / 兩餐^3 meals / 三餐^5 meals / 五餐~2~The price lies between 2 and 4. 價格介乎雙方機會成本之間。~
Markets, prices & property / 市場、價格與產權~A market is a group of buyers and sellers. In a market economy, decentralized choices coordinate through prices. Property rights support ownership and exchange; the invisible hand describes how self-interested choices can coordinate resource allocation under suitable conditions.~市場是買家與賣家的集合；市場經濟靠分散決策及價格協調資源。產權保障擁有和交易權利，價格傳遞稀缺與價值資訊。~Example 例：Coffee becomes scarce; a higher price encourages conservation and greater supply. Enforceable ownership lets sellers receive payment. 咖啡價格上升同時影響買賣雙方誘因。~Which signal coordinates buyers and sellers in a market economy? 什麼訊號協調市場？~A common price / 市場價格^A fixed production order for every firm / 統一產量命令^Equal incomes for all / 所有人同收入^The disappearance of property rights / 取消產權~0~Prices communicate relative scarcity and incentives. 價格把稀缺資訊傳給市場參與者。~
Efficiency & equality / 效率與平等~Efficiency concerns the size of total economic benefits from resources. Equality concerns their distribution. Redistribution may improve equality while changing incentives and the total surplus; the trade-off depends on the policy.~效率問總收益是否最大；平等問收益如何分配。重新分配可能改善分配，同時改變工作或投資誘因。~Example 例：A tax-transfer policy gives poorer households more income, but may reduce the reward to extra work. 轉移支付改善分配，也可能影響工作誘因。~A policy redistributes income while reducing total surplus. Which description fits? 重分配同時減少總剩餘？~Equality may improve, efficiency falls / 平等可能改善，效率下降^Both must improve / 兩者必改善^Efficiency rises by definition / 效率必升^Neither distribution nor incentives changes / 均無變~0~Distribution and total gains are separate criteria. 分配與總收益須分別判斷。~
Market failure / 市場失靈~Externalities affect people outside a transaction; market power lets a participant influence price. Both can prevent efficient allocation. A policy may instead be motivated by equality, or pursue both aims.~外部性影響交易以外的人；市場力量讓參與者影響價格。兩者可使市場結果偏離效率；其他政策可能主要關心收入分配。~Example 例：A smoke restriction protects bystanders, addressing an externality. Food vouchers target low incomes, mainly addressing equality. 禁煙與食物券的政策動機不同。~A rule reduces pollution imposed on nearby residents. Its direct efficiency rationale? 減少居民承受污染的效率理由？~Correcting an externality / 修正外部性^Eliminating every trade-off / 消除所有取捨^Guaranteeing equal profit / 保證同利潤^Increasing market power / 增加市場力量~0~The rule addresses costs borne by nonparticipants. 污染成本由交易外的第三方承受。~
