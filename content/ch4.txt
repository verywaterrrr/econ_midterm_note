Demand and supply laws / 供需法則~Quantity demanded is how much buyers are willing and able to buy at a given price; quantity supplied is what sellers are willing and able to sell. Holding other factors constant, higher price lowers Qd and raises Qs.~需求量是買家在某價願意及能夠購買的數量；供給量是賣家願意及能夠出售的數量。其他因素不變時，價格升令 Qd 降、Qs 升。~Example 例：At $5 buyers want 20 units; at $6 they want 16. This is a downward relationship between price and quantity demanded. 價格上升令需求量下降。~Holding other factors fixed, a higher price usually does what? 其他不變，價格上升？~Lowers Qd and raises Qs / Qd 降、Qs 升^Raises Qd and lowers Qs / Qd 升、Qs 降^Shifts both curves right / 均右移^Makes both quantities zero / 均歸零~0~This combines the laws of demand and supply.~market
Movement or shift / 沿線或移線~A good's own price moves the quantity along its existing demand or supply curve. A change in another determinant, such as income or input cost, shifts the relevant curve. State clearly which market is being studied.~本商品自身價格改變令數量沿現有曲線移動；收入、投入成本等其他因素改變才移動曲線。先確定分析哪一個市場。~Example 例：Coffee gets cheaper: move along coffee demand. Tea gets more expensive: coffee demand may shift right because tea is a substitute. 咖啡和茶價格對咖啡市場影響不同。~Coffee's own price falls. What happens to its demand curve, other things equal? 咖啡本身價格下降？~Move along the curve / 沿曲線移動^Shift demand right / 需求右移^Shift demand left / 需求左移^Shift supply left / 供給左移~0~Own-price changes affect quantity demanded along a fixed curve.~demandshift
Demand determinants / 需求因素~Income raises demand for normal goods and lowers demand for inferior goods. A substitute's higher price raises own demand; a complement's higher price lowers it. Tastes, expected future prices and buyer numbers can also shift demand.~收入增加令正常品需求增、劣等品需求減；替代品價格升令本品需求增，互補品價格升令本品需求減。喜好、預期及買家數也影響需求。~Example 例：Gasoline and cars are complements. More expensive gasoline can reduce demand for cars, shifting car demand left. 汽油更貴會降低汽車需求。~Tea substitutes for coffee. Tea price rises. Coffee demand? 茶價升時咖啡需求？~Increases / 增加^Decreases / 減少^Only moves along coffee demand / 只沿線^Must stay fixed / 必不變~0~Some buyers switch from tea to coffee at each coffee price.~demandshift
Supply determinants / 供給因素~Lower input costs, improved production technology or more sellers usually increase supply. Higher input costs reduce it. Expectations can alter current supply when production or storage can be shifted over time.~投入成本下降、技術改善或賣家增加通常令供給增加；成本升令供給減少。若能調整生產或存貨，未來價格預期也影響當期供給。~Example 例：Cheaper steel reduces the cost of making trucks; truck supply shifts right, lowering equilibrium price and raising quantity if demand is unchanged. 鋼價下降令卡車供給增加。~A new machine lowers unit production cost. Which shift is expected? 新機器降低成本？~Supply right / 供給右移^Supply left / 供給左移^Demand left / 需求左移^Only movement along supply / 只沿供給線移動~0~At each price, production becomes more attractive.~supplyshift
Market aggregation / 市場曲線加總~Add individual quantities at the same price. This is horizontal summation because quantity is on the horizontal axis. Do not add prices or include negative supplies or demands when an individual would choose zero.~在同一價格把各人的數量相加；因數量在橫軸，稱橫向加總。不可把價格相加；當個人選擇零時，不加入負數。~Example 例：At P=$4, A demands 3 units and B demands 5. Market demand is 3+5=8 units. 同價下加總數量。~At P=$10, three firms supply 2, 0 and 7 units. Market supply? 市場供給量？~7^9^10^19~1~2+0+7=9 at that price.~
Solving equilibrium / 求均衡~Equilibrium equates quantity demanded and supplied at one price. Solve the equations together, then substitute the price back into either equation and check both give the same quantity.~均衡要求同一價格下 Qd=Qs。聯立求 P，再代回兩式檢查數量一致。~Example 例：Qs=2P and Qd=300−P. 2P=300−P gives P=100 and Q=200. 兩式皆得到 200。~Qs=P, Qd=60−2P. Equilibrium P and Q? 均衡？~P=10,Q=10^P=20,Q=20^P=30,Q=30^P=60,Q=60~1~3P=60, so P=20 and Q=20.~market
Shortage and surplus / 短缺與過剩~Below equilibrium, Qd exceeds Qs and buyers compete for scarce supply, creating upward price pressure. Above equilibrium, Qs exceeds Qd and unsold stock creates downward pressure. A shortage or surplus is a difference between desired quantities.~低於均衡價時 Qd>Qs，有升價壓力；高於均衡價時 Qs>Qd，有降價壓力。短缺或過剩是意欲買賣數量的差額。~Example 例：At P=8, Qd=30 and Qs=18. Shortage=12; unrestricted price tends upward. 短缺十二單位。~At a given price Qs=50, Qd=35. What pressure occurs? 供五十、需三十五？~Surplus 15, price down / 過剩十五、價降^Shortage 15, price up / 短缺十五、價升^Surplus 85, price up / 過剩八十五、價升^Equilibrium / 均衡~0~Qs−Qd=15 units are unsold at that price.~shortage
One or two shifts / 單一及雙重移動~D rising raises both equilibrium P and Q; S rising lowers P and raises Q. Reverse shifts reverse those signs. If both curves move, combine the effects: an outcome is definite only when both shifts push it in the same direction.~D 增加令 P、Q 升；S 增加令 P 降、Q 升，反向移動結果相反。雙線移動時，只有兩個效果同向的變數能直接確定。~Example 例：D and S both rise. Each raises Q, so Q rises; their effects on P oppose, so P is ambiguous. 需求供給同增，量升而價未定。~D rises while S falls. What is definite? 需求增、供給減，何者確定？~P rises; Q ambiguous / 價升、量未定^Q rises; P ambiguous / 量升、價未定^P and Q both fall / 均下降^P falls; Q rises / 價跌量升~0~Both changes push price up; quantity effects oppose.~demandshift
Linked markets / 跨市場傳導~Identify the first market's shock and equilibrium response, then use related-good or input relationships to shift another market's curve. Keep movements along a curve separate from shifts in the next market.~先分析第一個市場的衝擊，再利用投入品、替代品或互補品關係傳到下一市場。每一步都重新辨認移動哪條線。~Example 例：Cheaper TV production raises TV supply, lowers TV price and increases demand for complementary streaming services. 電視便宜令串流服務需求增加。~Fuel costs rise for airlines; air travel and hotels are complements. Hotel demand likely? 航空燃料更貴，機票與酒店互補，酒店需求？~Falls / 下降^Rises / 上升^Hotel supply must rise / 酒店供給必升^Hotel demand becomes vertical / 需求變垂直~0~Costlier flights reduce travel and associated hotel demand, holding other factors fixed.~
Diagnosing observed changes / 從結果推斷衝擊~A higher P with higher Q is consistent with a demand increase alone; higher P with lower Q fits a supply decrease alone. These patterns identify possible single-shift explanations, but simultaneous shifts may also generate them.~價量同升可由需求增加解釋；價升量跌可由供給減少解釋。但多個因素同時改變也可有相同結果，不能把單線解釋當成唯一原因。~Example 例：Truck price rises while sales fall. More expensive steel shifting supply left fits this pattern. 鋼材成本上升可解釋價升量跌。~Price falls and quantity rises. Which single shift fits? 價跌量升符合哪個單一移動？~Supply increases / 供給增加^Supply decreases / 供給減少^Demand increases / 需求增加^Demand decreases / 需求減少~0~An outward supply shift moves equilibrium down the demand curve.~supplyshift
