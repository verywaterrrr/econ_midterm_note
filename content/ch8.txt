Reading a tax diagram / 讀稅收圖~Locate the original intersection at P0,Q0. After tax, read buyer price from demand and seller price from supply at the same lower quantity Qt. Their vertical difference is the unit tax.~先找稅前交點 P0、Q0。稅後在同一個較低交易量 Qt，從需求讀 Pb、供給讀 Ps；垂直差額就是單位稅。~Example 例：P0=100,Q0=200; after tax Pb=120,Ps=90,Qt=180. The wedge is 30 and quantity falls 20.~Tax diagram gives Pb=14,Ps=10 at Qt=50. Unit tax? 單位稅？~4^10^14^50~0~The tax wedge is 14−10=4 per unit, not total revenue.~tax
Revenue versus spending / 稅收、支出與收入~Government revenue is t times post-tax quantity. Buyers spend Pb×Qt, while sellers receive net receipts Ps×Qt. The difference between these two amounts is government tax revenue.~政府稅收是 t 乘稅後交易量；買方支出為 Pb×Qt，賣方淨收為 Ps×Qt，差額就是稅收。~Example 例：Pb=12,Ps=9,Q=100. Buyer spending=1,200; seller receipts=900; government revenue=300. 三個金額需分開。~t=5 and post-tax Q=80. Government revenue? 政府收入？~$16^$75^$400^$425~2~5×80=400. Use post-tax quantity.~
Post-tax welfare / 稅後福利~CS is computed using Pb, PS using Ps, and government revenue must be added to both. This counts tax payments as transfers within the standard welfare calculation rather than treating all tax as lost value.~CS 用 Pb，PS 用 Ps，再加政府收入。稅款在標準福利分析中是轉移，不能把整筆稅收當作損失。~Example 例：CS=120,PS=80,revenue=50. Total post-tax surplus=250. 三者相加。~After tax CS=40,PS=30,revenue=20. Total surplus? 稅後總剩餘？~50^70^90^110~2~40+30+20=90.~taxwelfare
Calculating DWL / 計算無謂損失~DWL is the lost surplus from trades prevented by the tax. With linear supply and demand and an interior solution, it is the triangle with height t and base Q0−Qt.~無謂損失來自稅令原本互利的交易消失。線性供需內部解下，三角形高為 t，底為減少的交易量。~Example 例：t=30, Q0=200, Qt=180. DWL=½×30×20=300. 單位稅乘減量再除二。~t=4, Q0=100, Qt=80. DWL? 無謂損失？~40^80^160^320~0~½×4×20=40.~tax
Lost surplus versus tax receipts / 剩餘損失與稅收~The reduction in CS plus PS equals government revenue plus DWL in the standard model. Revenue is transferred to government; DWL is the remaining loss in total surplus.~CS 與 PS 的總減少等於政府收入加 DWL。稅收只是轉到政府，超出稅收的損失才使總剩餘下降。~Example 例：Buyers and sellers jointly lose 500; government receives 350. DWL=500−350=150. 無謂損失為一百五十。~CS+PS falls by 120 and tax revenue is 90. DWL? 私人剩餘跌一百二十、稅收九十？~30^90^120^210~0~120−90=30 is the part not recovered as government revenue.~
Elasticity and DWL / 彈性與無謂損失~Holding the tax and relevant initial conditions fixed, more elastic supply or demand generally causes a larger quantity contraction. A wider base under the same wedge creates greater DWL.~單位稅與相關初始條件相同時，供給或需求更有彈性通常令數量減更多；同一高度下三角形底更大，DWL 更大。~Example 例：With t=10, a 5-unit fall gives DWL=25; a 15-unit fall gives DWL=75. 同稅而減量不同。~Same unit tax: market A loses 2 units, B loses 8. Which has larger linear-model DWL? 哪個 DWL 較大？~A^B^Equal / 相同^Neither can have DWL / 均無損失~1~The wedge height is equal, but B's lost-quantity base is larger.~
Zero DWL or zero revenue / 零損失與零稅收~Perfectly inelastic demand or supply can keep quantity unchanged despite a tax, yielding revenue with zero DWL. Conversely, a prohibitive tax can eliminate trade and raise zero revenue while destroying gains from the original trades.~完全無彈性的一方可令量不變，有稅收但無 DWL。相反，禁止性高稅可把交易降至零，無收入卻失去原本交易收益。~Example 例：A fixed 100 units still trade under a $2 tax: revenue=200 and DWL=0. If a different high tax prevents all beneficial trade, revenue=0 with positive DWL.~Can a tax raise zero revenue yet create positive DWL? 稅可無收入但有正 DWL？~Yes, if it eliminates beneficial trade / 可，若阻止互利交易^No, revenue and DWL are identical / 不可，兩者相同^Only if quantity rises / 只在量升^Only with a subsidy / 只限補貼~0~Zero tax receipts do not mean no lost trading opportunities.~
Buyer spending and seller receipts / 買方支出與賣方淨收~After a normal unit tax, Ps and Qt both fall, so seller net receipts fall for positive prices. Buyer spending can rise or fall because Pb rises while Q falls; use demand responsiveness or calculate the products.~一般單位稅後賣方淨價及數量皆降，正價格下淨收入下降。買方價升而量跌，支出可升可跌，要看彈性或直接計算。~Example 例：Before tax P=10,Q=100, spending=1,000. After Pb=12,Q=90, spending=1,080 even though CS falls. 支出增加不代表福利增加。~Buyer price rises and quantity falls after tax. Buyer spending is always lower? 買方支出必下降嗎？~Yes / 必下降^No; it depends on responses / 否，視反應而定^It must be zero / 必零^It equals PS / 等於 PS~1~Pb×Q combines opposite effects, so either direction is possible.~
Tax size and the Laffer curve / 稅額與拉弗曲線~A larger tax generally lowers quantity and raises DWL. Tax revenue equals t×Q(t), so the higher rate can eventually be outweighed by fewer transactions. With linear curves and interior trade, DWL grows quadratically in t.~加稅通常減少交易並增加 DWL；收入為 t×Q(t)，稅更高時數量收縮可能抵消稅額增加。線性內部解下 DWL 隨 t 的平方增加。~Example 例：If Q(t)=100−t, revenue=t(100−t); t=20 yields 1,600, t=50 yields 2,500, t=80 yields 1,600. 稅額太高可能少收。~For Q(t)=100−t, which tax maximizes revenue? 哪個稅額收入最高？~0^25^50^100~2~The revenue parabola peaks at t=50. This is not a claim that maximum revenue maximizes welfare.~laffer
Time horizon and taxes / 時間範圍與稅~Longer-run demand or supply may be more elastic as buyers switch technologies and sellers adjust capacity. With comparable initial conditions and the same tax, the larger long-run quantity response tends to raise DWL and reduce the taxed quantity. The slide-24 elasticity/Laffer extension is optional.~長期可換技術或調產能，供需常更有彈性。同稅及可比較初始條件下，長期減量可能更大、DWL 更高。第 24 張的拉弗延伸標作 optional。~Example 例：Heating-oil users cannot replace boilers immediately, but may switch fuels over several years. 長期避稅方式更多。~If the same tax causes a larger quantity reduction in year 5, DWL is generally? 同稅第五年減量更大？~Larger / 更大^Smaller / 更小^Necessarily zero / 必零^Equal to revenue / 等於稅收~0~The lost-quantity triangle has a larger base.~
Subsidy equilibrium / 補貼均衡~A unit subsidy creates Ps−Pb=s. Sellers receive more than buyers pay; supply and demand meet at those separate effective prices. In the ordinary competitive model, quantity rises and government spends s×Qsub.~補貼令 Ps 比 Pb 高 s。用兩個有效價格聯立供需；一般競爭模型下交易量增加，政府支出為 s 乘補貼後交易量。~Example 例：Qs=Ps,Qd=100−Pb,s=20. Ps=Pb+20 gives Pb=40,Ps=60,Q=60; government spends 1,200.~Subsidy per unit=3, traded quantity=50. Government spending? 政府支出？~$47^$53^$150^$300~2~3×50=150. Use the subsidized quantity.~subsidy
Subsidy welfare / 補貼福利~Subtract government spending from CS+PS. Without external benefits or another relevant distortion, the subsidy induces units whose marginal cost exceeds willingness to pay, causing DWL from overproduction.~總剩餘為 CS+PS 減政府支出。沒有外部效益等因素時，補貼誘使部分成本高於願付價的交易，造成過量生產損失。~Example 例：CS=80,PS=70,government spending=60. Total surplus=90. If original surplus was 100, DWL=10.~CS=60,PS=50,subsidy spending=30. Total surplus? 總剩餘？~20^80^110^140~1~60+50−30=80; spending is a cost in this calculation.~subsidy
Subsidy versus ceiling / 補貼與價格上限~A binding ceiling lowers the allowed price but restricts trade through shortage. A subsidy can lower buyer price while raising seller receipts and quantity, at a fiscal cost. Evaluate welfare using the stated externalities, allocation and government budget.~上限降低合法價格但造成短缺；補貼可同時降低買方價、提高賣方淨收及交易量，但要政府支出。政策評價取決於外部性、配給與財政假設。~Example 例：Rent ceiling discourages supply; a per-unit housing subsidy can encourage more supply while lowering tenant price. It requires funding. 租客便宜但兩種政策供給誘因不同。~Which typically requires direct government spending per unit traded? 何者通常每交易一單位需政府付款？~Binding ceiling alone / 單純價格上限^Per-unit subsidy / 每單位補貼^Nonbinding floor alone / 無約束下限^Removing a ceiling / 取消上限~1~The subsidy pays s for every traded unit; a ceiling itself does not.~
