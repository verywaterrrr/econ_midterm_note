Identify the price and units / 確認價格與單位~Write what each price measures before calculating: buyer payment, seller net receipt, worker wage or employer total cost. A unit tax has currency per unit; government revenue has currency.~先標出價格身份及單位：買方付款、賣方淨收、工人淨工資或企業勞工成本。單位稅為每件金額，政府收入則為總金額。~Example 例：Pb=12,Ps=9,Q=100. The unit wedge is $3 per unit; total revenue is $300. 每件三元與總三百元不同。~Employer cost=25, worker wage=20, hours=10. Total payroll tax? 工資稅總額？~5^10^50^250~2~(25−20)×10=50.~
Check curve versus movement / 檢查移線或沿線~Identify the market and the changed determinant. Own price changes quantity along a curve; another determinant can move the entire curve. Keep all stated “other things equal” assumptions in place.~先找市場及變動因素；自身價格改變沿線，其他決定因素改變可移線。題目指定其他不變的條件要保留。~Example 例：A rise in coffee price moves along coffee demand but can shift tea demand. 同一事件對不同市場的曲線影響不同。~A good's production cost falls. Which curve shifts in its market? 生產成本下降？~Supply / 供給^Demand solely because cost fell / 成本降直接需求移^Neither; only its own price changed / 只是自身價變^The PPF must vanish / PPF 消失~0~Cost is a supply determinant, not an own-price movement.~
Total, average or marginal / 總、平均或邊際~A total is an accumulated amount; an average divides a total by quantity; a marginal value measures the change from an additional unit. Match the measure to the question's decision.~總值是累積金額，平均值為總值除數量，邊際值是多一單位的變化。問是否增加一件，就使用額外收益及額外成本。~Example 例：TC(4)=40 and TC(5)=47. At q=5, ATC=9.4 but fifth-unit MC=7. 平均與新增成本不同。~TC rises from 100 to 118 when Q rises from 10 to 12. MC per extra unit over this interval? 每額外單位成本？~9^10^18^118~0~ΔTC/ΔQ=18/2=9.~
Traded quantity and graph assumptions / 交易量與圖形假設~Before measuring areas, establish actual traded quantity and the effective price paid or received. Under controls, the short side limits voluntary trade and allocation assumptions determine who gets the units.~畫面積前先確定售出量及有效價格。管制下自願交易取較小供需量；商品分給誰也會影響福利。~Example 例：Ceiling gives Qd=80,Qs=50. Use at most 50 traded units for welfare, then state which buyers obtain them. 需求八十不能當作已售八十。~With Qd=100,Qs=60 under a ceiling and no extra supply, maximum voluntary trades? 最多交易量？~40^60^100^160~1~The 60-unit supply is the short side.~
Private gains versus social welfare / 私人收益與社會福利~Separate changes in CS, PS and government revenue or spending before adding total surplus. A participant gaining money does not establish a social gain; transfers can change distribution without increasing total value.~分別算 CS、PS 及政府收入或支出，再加總。某方得益不等於社會總收益增加；轉移付款可只改變分配。~Example 例：After a subsidy CS rises 30, PS rises 20, government spends 60. Total surplus changes by 30+20−60=−10. 私人均得益但總剩餘減十。~CS rises 10,PS rises 15,government spends 40. Change in total surplus? 總剩餘變化？~+25^+65^−15^−40~2~10+15−40=−15.~
