Absolute advantage / 絕對優勢~Compare output for the same input, or input needed for the same output. The producer making more per hour, or requiring fewer hours per unit, has absolute advantage in that good.~同投入比較產量，或同產量比較投入。每小時產量較高或每件所需時間較少的一方有絕對優勢。~Example 例：A makes 8 shirts per hour; B makes 5. A has absolute advantage in shirts. 每小時八件高於五件。~A needs 2 hours per table; B needs 3. Who has absolute advantage? 誰有絕對優勢？~A^B^Both equally / 相同^Cannot compare / 無法比較~0~A uses fewer hours per table.~
Opportunity-cost ratios / 機會成本比率~For output-per-period data, cost of X in Y equals maximum Y divided by maximum X. For time-per-unit data, divide time for X by time for Y. The reverse opportunity cost is its reciprocal.~若資料是同期間產量，X 的成本為最大 Y 除最大 X；若資料是每件時間，則為製作 X 時間除製作 Y 時間。兩種商品的成本互為倒數。~Example 例：One pizza takes 2 hours and one drink takes 1 hour. A pizza costs 2 drinks; one drink costs 0.5 pizza. 比率互為倒數。~A can make 12 X or 4 Y daily. Opportunity cost of 1 Y? 每 Y 成本？~1/3 X^2 X^3 X^4 X~2~One Y uses a quarter of the day and sacrifices 3 X.~
Comparative advantage / 比較優勢~Comparative advantage belongs to the lower opportunity-cost producer. Even a producer with absolute advantage in both goods can gain from trade with someone whose relative costs differ. With two goods, one producer cannot have a strictly lower relative cost in both.~比較優勢看較低機會成本。即使一方兩種商品產能均較高，只要相對成本不同仍可互利交易；兩商品成本互為倒數，不能同時在兩者均有嚴格比較優勢。~Example 例：A makes 10 X or 20 Y; B makes 6 X or 6 Y. X costs A 2 Y and B 1 Y, so B has comparative advantage in X. B 的 X 相對成本較低。~Who has comparative advantage in Y in this example? 誰在 Y 有比較優勢？~A^B^Neither because A is faster at both / A 均較快所以沒有^Both strictly / 兩者均嚴格有~0~A gives up 0.5 X per Y; B gives up 1 X.~
Specialization & combined PPF / 分工與整體 PPF~Assign extra production first to the lowest opportunity-cost producer. Each exports the good with comparative advantage. Combining their production possibilities can generate a kink where the first producer becomes fully specialized.~增加某商品時先用機會成本最低的生產者。各方出口有比較優勢的商品；當第一位已完全專門化，再轉第二位，整體 PPF 斜率改變。~Example 例：A: 10 X or 20 Y; B: 6 X or 6 Y. Both all-Y gives (0,26); B switches fully to X gives (6,20); both all-X gives (16,0). 邊界依次經過三點。~In this example, the efficient specialized output with A all-Y and B all-X is? 分工後產量？~(X=6,Y=20)^(X=10,Y=6)^(X=16,Y=26)^(X=3,Y=10)~0~Add A's 20 Y and B's 6 X.~
Terms of trade / 貿易條件~For both to gain strictly, the exchange price of a good must lie between their opportunity costs, expressed in the same units. At an endpoint, one party is indifferent in the simple constant-cost model.~雙方嚴格得益的價格須介乎兩者機會成本，單位必須一致。在端點時，其中一方只是無差異。~Example 例：X costs its exporter 1 Y and importer 2 Y. Trading 1 X for 1.5 Y benefits both. 出口者收多於成本，進口者付少於自製代價。~Costs of 1 X are 2 Y and 5 Y. Which price gives strict gains to both? 哪個價格互利？~1 Y^2 Y^3 Y^6 Y~2~3 lies strictly between 2 and 5.~
Consumption after trade / 交易後消費~For each good, consumption equals production minus exports plus imports. Compare the resulting bundle with what the producer could make alone, or calculate the resources it would require without trade.~每種商品的消費等於產量減出口加進口。比較交易後組合與個人原本生產能力，可判斷是否獲益。~Example 例：B produces 6 X and exports 2 X for 3 Y; B consumes (4 X,3 Y). Its own PPF X+Y=6 cannot produce this total of 7. B 可消費超出原邊界的組合。~Produce 10 X, export 3 X and import 6 Y. Consumption? 生產十 X，出口三 X，進口六 Y？~(7 X,6 Y)^(10 X,6 Y)^(13 X,6 Y)^(3 X,6 Y)~0~Subtract exports and add imports good by good.~
Production versus consumption / 生產與消費~Trade changes consumption possibilities without automatically changing production technology. Individual consumption can lie outside an individual's PPF, while economy-wide production remains feasible and total consumption respects total available goods.~交易擴大個人可消費組合，但不會自動改變技術。個人消費可在自己的 PPF 外，整體生產仍受資源限制。~Example 例：A trades part of its specialized output for B's good. A consumes a mix it cannot efficiently produce alone, while aggregate goods still equal production. 交換重新分配既有商品。~A consumes outside its own PPF after trade. What follows? 交易後消費在個人 PPF 外，代表什麼？~Trade expanded consumption opportunities / 貿易擴大消費選擇^Its technology must have improved / 技術必改善^World production is infeasible / 世界生產不可行^Scarcity disappeared / 稀缺消失~0~A consumption gain does not require a technological shift.~trade
Productivity changes & trade / 生產力變化與貿易~Recalculate opportunity costs after productivity changes; absolute output growth alone does not determine trade patterns. Equal opportunity-cost ratios remove comparative-advantage gains in the simple two-good model.~生產力改變後要重算相對成本；只看產量上升不足以判斷貿易方向。兩方機會成本相同時，簡化模型沒有比較優勢收益。~Example 例：A makes 10 X or 20 Y; B improves from 6 X or 6 Y to 6 X or 12 Y. Both now give up 2 Y per X. B 的改善令相對成本一致。~Both countries now sacrifice 3 Y per X. In the simple model, what happens to comparative-advantage gains? 相對成本相同時？~They disappear / 消失^They must double / 必加倍^Only the bigger country gains / 只大國得益^Both export X necessarily / 必均出口 X~0~Equal relative costs leave no mutually advantageous price interval.~
