Models & evidence / 模型與證據~Models deliberately omit detail to isolate important relationships. Assumptions should fit the question and time horizon. Observations generate hypotheses, and evidence can support or challenge them.~模型透過假設省略細節，突出重要關係。假設應配合問題及時間範圍，理論須接受資料檢驗。~Example 例：A two-good economy isolates a production trade-off. Adding many goods may obscure this lesson without changing it. 兩商品模型令取捨更清楚。~Why use a simplified model? 為何簡化模型？~To isolate relevant mechanisms / 突出相關機制^To guarantee all predictions / 保證預測^To avoid evidence / 避免資料^To include every detail / 包括所有細節~0~Useful simplification focuses attention while retaining relevant relationships.~
Micro, macro & statements / 微觀、宏觀與陳述~Microeconomics studies individual choices and markets; macroeconomics studies aggregate outcomes. Positive claims can be tested against evidence; normative claims include a value judgment about what ought to happen.~微觀研究個人及市場，宏觀研究整體經濟。實證陳述原則上可檢驗；規範陳述涉及應該如何做的價值觀。~Example 例：“A tax reduces purchases” is positive; “The government should impose it” is normative. 稅是否減少購買可測試，應否徵稅含價值判斷。~Which is normative? 哪句是規範陳述？~Unemployment increased / 失業增加^Demand fell / 需求下降^Government should cut tax / 政府應減稅^Higher costs reduced supply / 成本升令供給減少~2~“Should” expresses a recommendation requiring values as well as facts.~
Why economists disagree / 經濟學家為何分歧~Economists can disagree about causal theories, assumptions, estimated responses or social values. Agreement on an effect does not guarantee agreement on whether a policy is desirable.~分歧可來自理論、假設、估計參數或價值觀。即使同意政策效果，也可能因對平等或效率的重視不同而不同意政策。~Example 例：Two economists agree a tax raises revenue, but disagree on how much labor supply falls and how important redistribution is. 對反應大小和分配價值均可有分歧。~Both agree on a policy's effects but prefer different outcomes. Main source of disagreement? 效果相同、偏好結果不同，分歧主要來自？~Values / 價值觀^Arithmetic error necessarily / 必定算錯^Different observed prices necessarily / 必定價格不同^No economic reasoning / 沒有經濟推理~0~Different normative weights can produce different recommendations.~
Circular flow / 經濟循環~Households supply factors to firms and buy goods from firms. Firms pay factor incomes and receive sales revenue. Real goods and inputs flow in the opposite direction to the associated money payments.~家庭提供勞工、土地及資本，並購買商品；企業購買要素並出售商品。實物流向與相應付款方向相反。~Example 例：You work in a bakery and receive wages through the factor market; buying bread uses the goods market. 你在餅店工作和買麵包屬不同市場。~In the factor market, households are usually what? 家庭在生產要素市場扮演什麼角色？~Sellers of inputs / 要素賣家^Buyers of final bread / 麵包買家^Only government agents / 政府代理^Sellers of all final goods / 所有成品賣家~0~Households own factors and supply them to firms. 家庭擁有並提供生產要素。~circular
PPF locations / PPF 位置判讀~Points on the PPF are productively efficient; points inside are feasible but inefficient. Points outside are infeasible with current resources and technology. Moving from an interior point toward the frontier can increase output without sacrificing the other good.~邊界上具生產效率，內部可行但有資源閒置或分配不當，外部在現有條件下不可達。由內部改善可同時增加產量。~Example 例：With X+Y≤10, (4,4) is interior, (4,6) is on the frontier, and (6,6) is outside. 三點分別代表無效率、效率、不可行。~If X+Y≤10, how is (3,5) classified? 如何分類？~Feasible and inefficient / 可行但無效率^Feasible and efficient / 可行有效率^Infeasible / 不可行^Only feasible after trade / 交易後才可行~0~3+5=8 leaves unused production capacity. 尚未用盡生產能力。~ppf
PPF opportunity cost / PPF 機會成本~The opportunity cost of one extra X is Y sacrificed divided by additional X. With Y on the vertical axis, it is the magnitude of the PPF slope. Always state units.~每多一單位 X 的機會成本是放棄的 Y 除以增加的 X。Y 在縱軸時，這是斜率的絕對值；答案要寫商品單位。~Example 例：From (2,10) to (4,4), ΔX=2 and Y lost=6. Cost of 1 X=3 Y. 每單位 X 放棄三單位 Y。~Moving from (X=1,Y=12) to (X=3,Y=8), cost per extra X? 每單位 X 成本？~0.5 Y^2 Y^4 Y^6 Y~1~Four Y are sacrificed for two X, so 4/2=2 Y per X.~ppf
PPF shape / PPF 形狀~A straight PPF has constant opportunity cost. A bowed-out PPF becomes steeper as more X is produced because resources transferred into X become less suitable for it relative to Y.~直線 PPF 代表固定機會成本；向外彎曲的 PPF 隨 X 增加而變陡，因資源專長不同，後來轉入 X 的資源代價較高。~Example 例：A specialist farmer switches first to farming; moving a skilled engineer into farming later may sacrifice much more engineering output. 依專長順序轉移資源，邊際代價會增加。~A bowed-out PPF normally implies what as X production expands? X 生產增加時通常怎樣？~Increasing cost in Y per X / 每 X 放棄更多 Y^Zero opportunity cost / 零成本^Constant cost everywhere / 各處成本固定^Unlimited output / 產量無限~0~Resources differ in suitability, producing increasing opportunity cost.~ppf
Moving versus shifting a PPF / 沿線與移線~A movement along the frontier reallocates existing resources. More resources or better technology can shift it outward. A technology improvement limited to X may raise its intercept while leaving the maximum Y unchanged.~沿 PPF 移動是既有資源重新分配；資源增加或技術進步令邊界外移。若只改善 X 技術，最大 Y 未必改變。~Example 例：Better computer technology raises possible computer output at many allocations; choosing more computers on the old frontier is simply reallocation. 技術進步與重新分配不同。~Only X technology improves. What may happen? 只有 X 技術改善？~X intercept rises; Y intercept can stay / X 截距升，Y 可不變^Both intercepts must fall / 均下降^Only a movement on old PPF / 只沿舊線移動^All interior points become infeasible / 內部均不可行~0~A good-specific technology change expands its production possibilities.~ppfshift
Economy-wide PPF / 整體 PPF~Allocate each good first to workers with the lowest opportunity cost. As different workers switch activity, the aggregate PPF can have kinks. Producing inside that boundary can reflect inefficient assignment, even if everyone is busy.~先安排機會成本最低的人做相應商品。不同工人依次轉換活動，整體邊界可有折點；人人忙碌仍可能因分工不當而無效率。~Example 例：A makes 10 X or 20 Y; B makes 6 X or 6 Y. Starting from all Y, move B to X first: it sacrifices 1 Y per X, versus A's 2. 先轉 B 的代價較低。~From all-Y production, who should switch to X first in the example? 誰應先轉做 X？~A / 甲^B / 乙^Both must switch together / 必須同時^The choice never matters / 永不影響~1~B has the lower opportunity cost of X.~
Peace dividend on a bowed PPF / 彎曲 PPF 的轉產收益~On a bowed PPF, the opportunity cost of the horizontal good is greater where much of it is already produced. Giving up the same amount of that good can therefore release different quantities of the other good at different locations.~向外彎曲邊界上，已大量生產橫軸商品時，其邊際機會成本較高。因此在不同起點放棄同量該商品，可換回不同數量另一商品。~Example 例：With guns on X, shifting 1 unit away from guns near the high-guns end releases more butter than near the low-guns end. 在高軍備端減少一單位軍備可增加較多奶油。~Same small cut in guns on a bowed PPF: where is the butter gain generally larger? 同量減軍備，哪處奶油收益較大？~High-guns end / 高軍備端^Low-guns end / 低軍備端^Always identical / 必相同^Always zero / 必為零~0~The PPF is steeper at the high-guns end. 高軍備端斜率絕對值較大。~ppf
Axes, slope and shifts / 座標、斜率與移動~Read the axis labels before interpreting a graph. Slope is Δy/Δx; horizontal lines have zero slope and vertical slopes are undefined. A changed variable on an axis usually moves a point along a fixed relationship; another determinant can shift it.~先辨認橫縱軸及單位。斜率是縱向變化除橫向變化；水平為零，垂直斜率未定義。軸上的變數改變與整條關係移動須分清。~Example 例：From (Q=2,P=8) to (Q=6,P=4), slope=(4−8)/(6−2)=−1 dollars per unit. 斜率為每單位數量負一元。~From (x=1,y=7) to (x=4,y=1), slope? 斜率多少？~−2^−0.5^+2^+6~0~Δy/Δx=−6/3=−2.~
Correlation & causation / 相關與因果~A correlation can reflect a causal effect, an omitted common cause or reverse causality. A graph alone generally cannot distinguish these explanations. Ask what variable is missing and which direction of influence is plausible.~相關可能來自因果、共同遺漏因素或反向因果。單看圖形通常無法分辨；要問是否漏了因素，或因果方向倒轉。~Example 例：Cities with more crime hire more police. A positive police–crime correlation need not mean police cause crime. 高犯罪可引致增聘警察。~More lighters correlate with cancer; smoking is unmeasured. Main concern? 打火機與癌症相關但未量度吸煙？~Omitted variable / 遺漏變數^Proof lighters cause cancer / 已證明因果^A normative statement / 規範陳述^A guaranteed random experiment / 隨機實驗~0~Smoking can increase both lighter ownership and cancer risk.~
