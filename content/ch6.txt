Binding controls / 有約束力的管制~A ceiling is a legal maximum and binds below the free-market equilibrium price. A floor is a legal minimum and binds above equilibrium. A control on the other side of equilibrium leaves the equilibrium available.~價格上限在均衡價以下才具約束力；價格下限在均衡價以上才具約束力。若原均衡仍合法，市場可留在原均衡。~Example 例：P*=100. A ceiling of 90 binds; a floor of 90 does not. 同樣九十元，上限與下限效果不同。~Equilibrium price is $20. Which control binds? 均衡價二十元，何者具約束力？~Ceiling $25 / 上限二十五^Floor $15 / 下限十五^Ceiling $15 / 上限十五^Floor $20 with no excess / 下限二十且無過剩~2~A $15 maximum prevents the market reaching its $20 equilibrium.~ceiling
Traded quantity under controls / 管制下交易量~Calculate Qd and Qs separately at the controlled price. A shortage is Qd−Qs; a surplus is Qs−Qd. With voluntary trade and no government buying or extra supply, actual trade is limited by the smaller quantity.~把管制價分別代入供需，短缺為 Qd−Qs，過剩為 Qs−Qd。自願交易且政府不額外買賣時，實際交易量取較小數。~Example 例：At a floor, Qd=40 and Qs=70. Surplus=30, but only 40 units are sold. 想出售七十不代表售出七十。~At a ceiling Qd=90 and Qs=60. Quantity sold and shortage? 上限下需九十、供六十？~Sold 60, shortage 30 / 售六十、缺三十^Sold 90, shortage 30 / 售九十、缺三十^Sold 30, shortage 60 / 售三十、缺六十^Sold 150, shortage 0 / 售一百五十、無缺~0~The supply side limits trade to 60 units.~ceiling
Rationing and quality / 配給與品質~A binding ceiling creates excess demand, so price no longer alone selects buyers: queues, waiting lists or favoritism may emerge. Sellers can also reduce quality. A binding floor leaves some willing sellers unable to sell.~上限造成需求過多，可能靠排隊、名單或偏好分配；賣家也可能減少維修或品質。下限造成過剩，部分賣家無法出售。~Example 例：Under rent control, apartments have waiting lists and landlords may reduce maintenance when rent cannot reflect quality. 租金限制可改變維修誘因。~A binding rent ceiling may produce which result? 有約束力的租金上限可能？~Waiting lists and lower maintenance / 等候名單、維修減少^Guaranteed housing for every applicant / 人人必有屋^Excess vacant supply from the ceiling alone / 單靠上限造成過剩^Automatic efficient rationing / 自動有效率配給~0~The shortage requires non-price allocation; quality incentives can weaken.~ceiling
Minimum wage and employment / 最低工資與就業~Workers supply labor and firms demand it. At a binding minimum wage, employment equals labor demanded and unemployment in this simple diagram equals labor supplied minus labor demanded.~工人供應勞工，企業需求勞工。最低工資高於均衡時，就業取企業需求量，圖中的失業為勞工供給減需求。~Example 例：At the wage floor, 120 workers want jobs and firms hire 80. Employment=80 and unemployment=40. 想工作的人不等於成功就業者。~At the minimum wage Ls=100 and Ld=70. Employment? 就業人數？~30^70^100^170~1~Firms hire 70; the remaining 30 willing workers lack jobs in this model.~floor
Wage bill and elasticities / 工資總額與彈性~Total wage payments equal wage times employment. Along inelastic labor demand, a wage rise reduces employment proportionately less, raising the wage bill. Unemployment changes depend on both labor demand and labor supply responses.~總工資為工資乘就業。勞工需求缺乏彈性時，工資升幅超過就業跌幅，總支付上升；失業變化則同時涉及供給與需求。~Example 例：Wage 10→12 and employment 100→95: total payments 1,000→1,140. 勞工總收入上升，但五人失去工作。~A small wage increase on elastic labor demand does what to total wage payments? 有彈性勞工需求下小幅加薪？~Raises them / 增加^Lowers them / 減少^Always leaves them unchanged / 必不變^Equals unemployment / 等於失業~1~The employment percentage decline exceeds the wage percentage rise.~
Short-run versus long-run shortage / 短長期短缺~At a fixed rent ceiling, more elastic long-run supply can reduce housing offered more, while more elastic demand raises desired quantity more. Together these can enlarge the shortage relative to the short run.~租金上限固定時，長期供給更能收縮，需求更能增加，短缺因而可能比短期更大。比較時要固定其他條件及原均衡。~Example 例：Short run Qd=110, Qs=90 gives shortage 20; long run Qd=130, Qs=70 gives shortage 60. 長期調整令短缺擴大。~With the same binding ceiling and more responsive supply and demand, shortage generally? 同上限、供需更有彈性？~Grows / 增大^Must vanish / 必消失^Becomes negative always / 必變負^Equals tax revenue / 等於稅收~0~Both responses can widen Qd−Qs.~ceiling
Rationing and surplus / 配給與剩餘~Surplus depends on who receives or supplies the traded units. The usual maximum-surplus areas under a control assume high-value buyers receive goods and low-cost sellers supply them. Random or discriminatory allocation can reduce surplus further.~管制後的剩餘取決於誰成功買賣。常見圖形面積假設商品到最高價值買家、由最低成本賣家供應；隨機或偏好配給可再減少剩餘。~Example 例：One unit costs $2. At a $3 ceiling, allocating it to a buyer valuing it at $10 gives total surplus $8; to a $5 buyer gives $3. 同交易量，配給不同令剩餘不同。~Under a shortage, can exact CS always be calculated without knowing who buys? 不知誰買到時可必定算出 CS？~Yes, price alone fixes CS / 價格已足夠^No, allocation matters / 配給重要^Yes, Qd is quantity sold / Qd 就是售量^No, because CS is always zero / CS 必零~1~Buyer valuations among actual recipients determine CS.~welfare
Drawing a unit tax / 畫單位稅~A tax on sellers shifts supply vertically up by t in buyer-price terms. A tax on buyers shifts demand vertically down by t in seller-price terms. Both create the same gap Pb−Ps=t under the standard model.~向賣方收稅，可在買方價格座標把供給上移 t；向買方收稅，可在賣方價格座標把需求下移 t。結果均是 Pb 比 Ps 高 t。~Example 例：If sellers need net $10 to supply a quantity, a $3 tax means buyers must pay $13 for that quantity. 淨收十元需要買方付十三元。~A $4 unit tax on sellers shifts supply by how much vertically? 賣方每件四元稅？~Up $4 / 上移四元^Down $4 / 下移四元^Right 4 units / 右移四件^No wedge / 沒有稅楔~0~At each quantity, the buyer price must cover the old supply price plus tax.~tax
Tax equilibrium / 稅後均衡~Use separate price variables: demand depends on Pb and supply on Ps. Solve Qd(Pb)=Qs(Ps) together with Pb−Ps=t. Substituting a single price into both original curves without the wedge loses the tax.~需求用買方價 Pb，供給用賣方淨價 Ps，再聯立 Pb−Ps=t。不可在原供需兩式都用同一價格而漏掉稅楔。~Example 例：Qs=2Ps, Qd=300−Pb, t=30. Pb=Ps+30 gives 2Ps=270−Ps; Ps=90, Pb=120, Q=180.~Qs=Ps, Qd=100−Pb and t=20. Seller price? 賣方淨價？~20^40^50^60~1~Ps=100−(Ps+20), so Ps=40; Pb=60 and Q=40.~tax
Per-unit tax burden / 每單位稅負~Compare each post-tax price with the original equilibrium P0. Buyers bear Pb−P0, sellers bear P0−Ps, and these add to t. Total burdens across traded units multiply these amounts by the post-tax quantity.~買方每件負擔 Pb−P0，賣方每件負擔 P0−Ps，合計為 t。要算交易單位上的總負擔，再乘稅後數量。~Example 例：P0=100, Pb=120, Ps=90. Buyers bear 20 and sellers 10; total unit tax=30. 買方負擔三分之二。~P0=50, Pb=56, Ps=46. Seller's burden per unit? 賣方每件負擔？~4^6^10^46~0~50−46=4. The remaining 6 is borne by buyers.~tax
Elasticity and incidence / 彈性與稅負~The relatively less elastic side has fewer effective alternatives, so it typically absorbs more of a unit tax. Legal responsibility for remitting the tax does not determine the economic burden.~相對缺乏彈性的一方較難調整，通常承擔較多稅負。法定把錢交政府的一方與最終承擔負擔的一方須分清。~Example 例：If supply is very elastic while demand is steep, buyers bear most of a small tax. 供給容易退出，買家較難避開稅負。~Demand is perfectly inelastic and supply slopes up. Who bears the unit tax? 需求垂直、供給向上，誰承擔？~Buyers / 買家^Sellers / 賣家^Always half each / 必各半^Whoever remits it / 依法定繳稅方~0~Quantity stays fixed and the buyer price absorbs the wedge in this model.~
Payroll taxes / 工資稅~Treat labor as the traded service. Firms are buyers paying total labor cost; workers are sellers receiving net wages. The gap equals the payroll tax. Changing which side legally pays an unchanged total tax does not by itself alter incidence.~把勞工當交易服務，企業支付總勞工成本，工人收到淨工資；差額是工資稅。總稅不變時，改由哪一方繳稅本身不改變經濟歸宿。~Example 例：Employer cost $110, worker net wage $95: payroll tax wedge=$15. 企業成本和工人淨收不能混用。~Employer pays $120 per hour and worker receives $100. Tax wedge? 工資稅楔多少？~$10^$20^$100^$120~1~120−100=$20 per hour.~
